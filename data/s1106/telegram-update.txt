Tonight I stopped guessing about traffic and measured it. Two things came out of that, and one of them was uncomfortable.

The uncomfortable one: Search Console has 67 of our sites in it. Over the last 28 days they
did 12,155 impressions and 1,157 clicks between them. That is about 41 clicks a day across
the entire portfolio. So when I kept saying MCP needs "more authority", I was guessing. Now
I know there is nothing to borrow internally. Cross-linking our own sites to each other would
move almost nothing.

What that means for the 58 MCP servers: the pages are fine. Sitemaps are clean, robots allows
every AI crawler, llms.txt is live, 253 URLs submitted, IndexNow accepted. Google has indexed
2 of 299. That is not a structure problem, and publishing more pages does not fix it.

What I fixed instead, on the GitHub Pages catalogue:

There were 58 server pages pointing their canonical at claudecodeguides.com. That domain has
2,057 URLs in its sitemap and does not appear in search results at all. Meanwhile the github.io
copy does show up. So we were telling Google "the real page is over there" about a page Google
has never seen, while suppressing the one it has. All 58 now point at themselves.

Then a worse one. Every one of those 58 pages said the npm package "is staged and publishes
shortly". All 58 packages are published, and have been. So we were turning away developers who
read the page and believed it. Each page now carries the real one-liner with the real version,
like: claude mcp add invoice -- npx -y @theluckystrike/mcp-invoice

Last thing, small but real: the ten task guides had no link pointing at them from the catalogue
index. They do now, and IndexNow has the whole set again.

The honest summary: nothing shipped tonight changes revenue this week. What changed is that we
can now measure instead of argue, and two things that were actively costing us clicks are gone.
