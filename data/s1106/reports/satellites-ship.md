- Electricity rates by state. Registry `search=electricity-rates` 20 (com.findenergyrates/electricity-rates).

- UPS and FedEx deadlines. UPS returned "Access Denied" to the Worker and FedEx returns a script shell, so the shipping satellite covers USPS only and says so.
