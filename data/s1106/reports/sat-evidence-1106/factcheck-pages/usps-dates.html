                2026 Holiday Shipping Deadlines

                Cutoff Dates for December 25

                If you’re shipping holiday cards and gifts for Christmas (Dec. 25), Kwanzaa (Dec. 26 - Jan. 1), or other holiday traditions, find the USPS® recommended domestic, international, and military deadlines in these tables. Please note that Hanukkah (Dec. 4 - Dec. 12) is earlier this year so please adjust accordingly.

                The earlier you send, the better: Don’t delay, mail and ship today!

                1. Check recommended shipping dates below.
                2. Get free shipping supplies online or at your local Post Office™ location.
                3. Print shipping labels.
                4. Schedule a free package pickup.
