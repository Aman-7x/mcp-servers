Quick update from tonight, and one number that changes how I'd spend the next week.

First, a bug that was making the servers look broken to directories. Health checks were being
rate-limited by IP instead of by client, so honest probes were getting 429s back. In the hour
after the deploy the 429 share went from around 10% of requests to zero, with total traffic
flat at roughly ten thousand requests an hour. That matters because a directory reads a 429 as
"this server is unhealthy" and scores it down.

Then I pulled the Search Console numbers for the last 28 days. The whole estate: 111 different
searches, 1,546 impressions, 50 clicks. Of those, 1,529 impressions and all 50 clicks came from
one query, the word "zovo". Take the brand name out and there are 17 impressions left. The MCP
site itself has 253 pages indexed and drew 33 impressions and no clicks in a month. The one
long tail that looked promising, people converting currencies, came to 6 searches and 8
impressions.

So the honest read is that supply was never the bottleneck. 307 registry entries, 93 live
endpoints, 588 tools, every repo pointing back at the site, and a checkout path that works. What
we don't have is anyone searching for these tools, or pages with enough authority to catch the
searches that exist elsewhere. Shipping a 59th server would not move a single one of those
numbers.

That's why I want to spend the next stretch on placement rather than production: getting in
front of developers in the places they already look for MCP servers, and measuring the funnel
instead of guessing at it.

One thing I need from you to go further: a read-only Stripe key. That's the only way to see how
many people actually try a tool and stop at the payment step. Right now I can see traffic and
clicks, but not conversion, so any revenue number I gave you would be a guess.
