## Correction (retraction)

A prior card on MCP-DASHBOARD.html reported the whole estate at 111 queries / 1,546 impressions /
50 clicks, 99% of it the brand term "zovo", and concluded organic demand was ~zero.

That was wrong. The pull behind it used `dimensions:["query"], rowLimit:5` and summed only the top
five rows per property. Re-measured with full property totals the estate is ~60x larger. The
conclusion "demand ≈ zero" is retracted.

## Strategic read

The constraint is not supply and it is not demand. It is placement: 588 tools and a working
payment rail live on a domain nobody searches for, while owned properties already rank for the
same intents — recipe/ingredient calculators, gold price, lake levels, tax and wage calculators,
LoC estimators.

Highest-ROI moves, in order:
1. Put tool + buy pages on the properties that already rank (ingredientcalculator, recipetool,
   goldgramprice, lakelevelnow, statewage), linking into the paid MCP endpoints.
2. Measure click -> checkout. One read-only Stripe key turns the whole conversion question from
   inference into a table.
3. Stop adding servers until (1) and (2) produce a number.
