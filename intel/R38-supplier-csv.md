## User behavior

Choose two UTF-8 files and declare their common as-of date. Both files are accepted completely or rejected completely. Exact eight-column headers may be reordered but not renamed, added or duplicated. Quoted commas/quotes, one initial BOM, Unicode and leading-zero IDs/references are retained. Malformed UTF-8, ragged/blank rows, invalid last rows and noncanonical positive minor-unit amounts are refused. No header/value mapping, trimming, currency/date conversion, row skipping or inferred pairing occurs. The unchanged product schema remains authoritative.
