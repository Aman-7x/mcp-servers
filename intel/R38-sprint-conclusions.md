# What the accumulated MCP sprints have established

This assessment uses the 26 October native revenue and acquisition audit. Older sprint reports remain dated historical evidence. A listing accepted in September is not proof of a buyer in October, and source tests are not paid fulfillment.

The largest established engineering win is a complete, inspectable path from a useful local task to a distributable package and a scoped paid key. It now includes useful free evidence, immutable versioned downloads, a clean standalone Office installation, live intent forms and independent hosted product/bundle access. Earlier sprints built the platform; R38 closes concrete gaps that could frustrate an interested user or remove purchased access. This is delivery and reliability value. Its monetary return has not been measured.

The largest commercial insight is that the accumulated supply has not established demand. All available completed MCP Checkout history contains zero paid matches and zero gross, independently checked by storefront host, product metadata and known price IDs. Finalized Google Search shows 38 impressions and zero clicks. The seven-date hosted instrument has19unmarked successful responses and zero handoffs; those responses are not identified people, completed buyer tasks or a ranked per-server audience. More server names, aliases, clones and automated directory scores cannot resolve that gap by themselves.

The earlier September sprint-loop report described Registry-to-Glama indexing and accepted distribution submissions as leading indicators. Those receipts remain useful evidence that owned listings can propagate. Its claims that conversion measurement was the only remaining constraint and npm login the biggest unlock were hypotheses at that date. The current revenue and acquisition audit does not validate them. R38 therefore publishes a narrow supplier-statement workflow, provides a free browser task before installation and directs relevant owned entry points to that task.

The next commercial decision should use actual requested trials with relevant supplier/AP exports, voluntary repeat use and an independent payment. Ten trials, three repeats and one payment are proposed management thresholds, not a forecast or an achieved result. Ask each participant what they did with the exception evidence, whether they returned with another batch and what saved-report capacity would replace in their existing process. Keep complete diagnostic evidence free while testing that paid need.
